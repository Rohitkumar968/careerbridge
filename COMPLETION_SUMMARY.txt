## 🔄 State Management

11 Redux Slices:
- authSlice (authentication)
- themeSlice (dark/light mode)
- jobSlice (jobs and filtering)
- applicationSlice (applications)
- notificationSlice (notifications)
- interviewSlice (interviews)
- resumeSlice (resume data)
- recruiterSlice (recruiter data)
- candidateSlice (candidates)
- adminSlice (admin data)
- aiSlice (AI features)

- jobApi (CRUD, search, recommendations)
- applicationApi (apply, track, withdraw)
- resumeApi (upload, analyze, download)
- interviewApi (schedule, reschedule, cancel)
- recruiterApi (company, applicants, analytics)
- aiApi (resume analysis, job matches, chat)
- adminApi (users, jobs, companies, reports)
